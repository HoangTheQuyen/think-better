# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **hoangthequyen01@gmail.com**.
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may
remove comments, commits, or contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors who violate this code, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
